inspirations.

01 · the idea

DAMN. → MN.

the idea for the MN. logo comes from the cover of DAMN. by kendrick lamar. one short word in capitals that doesn't explain itself, it just stands there.

that's what i wanted for MN.: my initials, and the rest is the work. at one point i even wanted ZE DAMN 1 as my licence plate. my car isn't cool enough for that yet.

02 · the font

roman capitals

i always had something for this kind of font, because of the romans. capital letters with thick and thin lines that look like they were cut into stone and made to last.

that's the feeling i want MN. to have.

03 · the dot

the dot.

the dot comes from harry styles. on twitter he had a dot after his name, and i always thought it stood out. a name, a dot, done.

so MN. got one too.

04 · the sound

blonde.

blonde by frank ocean. the cover is a photo and one small word in the corner: blond. no big logo, nothing loud. it doesn't need more.

his music does something to me that's hard to explain. it's quiet and honest, it doesn't try to impress anyone, and it still hits harder than most loud things. that's how i want MN. to feel.

so the big words on this site are set in a clean, round font in that spirit. less noise, more feeling.

05 · the teasers

who's that?

frank ocean's last four singles inspired my teasers: dhl, in my room, dear april and cayendo.

a shape, one word, a colour. SOON. TOMORROW. you see the outline and have to guess what's coming, like “who's that pokémon?” when we were kids.

i started doing it in lavendel. the ones from ber. are in the chapter.

digitals.

no styling, no editing. natural light, as i look right now.

faq.

you call yourself a model. you must be arrogant and think highly of yourself.

no. i just stopped waiting for permission. MN. isn't about thinking i'm better than anyone, it's about becoming a better version of me, and showing that you can do that too.

i didn't start because i thought i was perfect. i started because i wasn't sure of myself, and i wanted to change that. MN. is me working on myself, in public, one colour at a time. calling yourself something is the first step to becoming it. if that helps one person like themselves a bit more, it was worth it.

why do you do this?

to grow, and to show that you can start from wherever you are.

and for my grandma. she was still here at the beginning and saw the baby blue. photos before they were ever posted. she was proud of me. that's something i carry into every chapter.

what does MN. stand for?

markus nindl. it started on 01.01.2025 as a small page and became the name for everything i create.

the logo is inspired by the cover of DAMN. by kendrick lamar. at one point i even wanted ZE DAMN 1 as my licence plate, but my car isn't cool enough for that yet.

what are the colours about?

every chapter of my life gets a colour. each one is a time, a place and a version of me, from origin. to red. the colours tell the story, so you don't need me to explain every detail.

what does “i mog di” mean?

it's pinzgau dialect for “i like you”, with a second meaning.

“mog” comes from the looksmaxxing scene, where “mogging” means looking better than someone. looksmaxxing is about getting the most out of yourself: skin, fitness, style, sleep, how you carry yourself. that inspired me. i believe in hypergamy, but i don't compare myself to others. the only person i try to mog is who i was yesterday.

so “i mog di” means both: like yourself, and keep working on yourself. and if i can help someone deal with their insecurities along the way, even better.

do you do this full-time?

no. i work full-time at the österreichische post ag, where i'm the deputy site manager (stellvertretender standortleiter). MN. happens before, after and in between, and that's part of the story.

why pinzgau and not vienna or munich?

my best friend told me i belong in the city. maybe. but i like trying something new in a place where it's uncommon to do it.

this is home. you don't need a big city to start something.

why is your instagram private?

because MN. is a geheimtipp. it stays private until 1,000 followers. if you found this page, you're early.

why do you wear tracht to every event?

it was my idea for 2026: to stand out, and because it's austria. i also like the idea of wearing your best clothes every day, not only on special occasions.

what was your first runway?

the s.oliver fashion show at intersport harms in neukirchen, on 30.10.2026.

what's your goal?

after being single des monats in the platzhirsch at the end of 2024, the next one is model des monats.
