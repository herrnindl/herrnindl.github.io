inspirations.

01 · the idea

DAMN. → MN.

the idea for the MN. logo comes from the cover of DAMN. by kendrick lamar. one short word in capitals. it doesn't explain itself, it just stands there.

that's what i wanted for MN.: my initials, and the rest is the work. at one point i even wanted ZE DAMN 1 as my licence plate. my car isn't cool enough for that yet.

02 · the font

roman capitals.

i've always had a thing for this kind of font, because of the romans. capital letters with thick and thin lines that look like they were cut into stone and made to last.

part of that comes from assassin's creed. my brother got me to play the first one when it came out in 2007. old cities, history, names carved in stone. i was a kid, and that probably had a bigger impact on me than i knew back then.

that's the feeling i want MN. to have.

03 · the dot

the dot.

the dot comes from harry styles. on twitter he had a dot after his name, and i always thought it stood out. a name, a dot, done.

so MN. got one too.

04 · the sound

blonde.

blonde by frank ocean. the cover is just a photo with one small word in the corner. no big logo, nothing loud. it doesn't need more.

his music does something to me that's hard to explain. it's quiet and honest, it doesn't try to impress anyone, and it still hits harder than most loud things. that's how i want MN. to feel.

that's where my second logo comes from: nindl. small, lowercase, in the corner of a photo. the big words on this site use the same clean, round font. less noise, more feeling.

05 · the teasers

who's that?

the last four singles by frank ocean inspired my teasers: dhl, in my room, dear april and cayendo.

a shape, one word, a colour. SOON. TOMORROW. you see the outline and have to guess what's coming, like “who's that pokémon?” when we were kids.

i started doing it in lavendel. the ones from ber. are in the chapter.

06 · the vanilla

vanilla.

vanilla. comes from @prettybookplaces. her highlights and the way she makes content were the inspiration for the whole chapter.

she's also the reason bodoni stayed my main font.

07 · the colours

the colours.

every chapter has its own colour. this is where they came from.

  • ember.came after the announcement of the wildkogel sundaze instagram page.
  • ber.explains itself: septem·ber to decem·ber. it's also the colour of my eyes.
  • vanilla.came from @prettybookplaces.
  • grey. white. origin.came from the idea to make fewer story highlights and put them in order, a week or two before i started working on this website.
  • navy blue. lavendel.came from chatgpt. i gave it the first photo on my homepage, the one in my leather jacket, and asked which colours suit me best.
  • baby blue.came from the canadian tuxedo.

digitals.

no styling, no editing. natural light, as i look right now.

faq.

you call yourself a model. you must be arrogant and think highly of yourself.

no. i just stopped waiting for permission. MN. isn't about thinking i'm better than anyone, it's about becoming a better version of me, and showing that you can do it too.

i didn't start because i thought i was perfect. i started because i wasn't sure of myself, and i wanted to change that. MN. is me working on myself, in public, one colour at a time. calling yourself something is the first step to becoming it. if that helps one person like themselves a bit more, it was worth it.

why do you do this?

to grow, and to show that you can start from wherever you are.

and for my grandma. she was still here at the beginning and saw the baby blue. photos before they were ever posted. she was proud of me. that's something i carry into every chapter.

what are the colours about?

every chapter of my life gets a colour. each one is a time, a place and a version of me, from origin. to now. the colours tell the story, so you don't need me to explain every detail.

what does “i mog di” mean?

it's pinzgau dialect for “i like you”, with a second meaning.

“mog” comes from the looksmaxxing scene, where “mogging” means looking better than someone. looksmaxxing is about getting the most out of yourself: skin, fitness, style, sleep, how you carry yourself. that inspired me. i don't compare myself to others. the only person i try to mog is who i was yesterday.

so “i mog di” means both: like yourself, and keep working on yourself. and if i can help someone deal with their insecurities along the way, even better.

do you do this full-time?

no. i work full-time at the österreichische post ag, where i'm the deputy site manager (stellvertretender standortleiter). MN. happens before, after and in between, and that's part of the story.

why pinzgau and not vienna or munich?

my best friend told me i belong in the city. maybe. but i like doing something new in a place where nobody else does it.

this is home. you don't need a big city to start something.

why is your instagram private?

because MN. is still a geheimtipp, an insider tip. it stays private until 1,000 followers. if you found this page, you're early.

what's your goal?

i was single des monats in the platzhirsch at the end of 2024. next up: model des monats.

can i book you?

yes. shootings, campaigns, fashion shows and collaborations. write to booking@herrnindl.com with a short idea, date and place.

unpaid or tfp shoots? yes, if the photographer is cool or good. send me your work and your idea.
